About

A calm, confidential space for your growth.

Thomas Oluwaseun G. is a licensed counselor dedicated to mental wellness and academic growth, providing professional and compassionate counseling to students and members of the community.

He holds an NCE, a B.Ed in Counseling Psychology and Human Development, and an M.Ed in Counseling and Education Psychology, and is a certified member of the Counselling Association of Nigeria (CASSON).

Counseling sessions are voluntary and confidential — a calm space to navigate life's complexities, explore your path to clarity, and build lasting resilience.

Qualifications & certification

  • B.Ed Counseling Psychology & Human Development

Take care of your mind

Anger management
  • • Pause and breathe
  • • Count to 10
  • • Talk it out, don't blow it out
  • • Walk away if you need to
Handling depression
  • • Talk to someone you trust
  • • Stay connected
  • • Do one small thing each day
  • • Seek professional help
Memory & concentration
  • • Get enough sleep
  • • Eat well and stay hydrated
  • • Take breaks while studying
  • • Practice, repeat, review
